# Effect of temperature on catalase activity (synthetic report)

Written for a test; not a real student report.

## Introduction

Catalase breaks down hydrogen peroxide. Enzyme activity depends on temperature (Levi, 2018). We hypothesised an optimum near 30.

## Methods

We measured O₂ production at five temperatures, three replicates each.

## Results

| Temperature | Activity |
|---|---|
| 10 | 4.1 |
| 30 | 12.2 |
| 50 | 2.3 |

Activity peaked at 30, which shows that the enzyme denatures above body temperature because its hydrogen bonds break.

## Discussion

The experiment proved our hypothesis.
