# Introduction to Probability for Engineering — syllabus (excerpt)

## Exam

- Final exam, three hours, closed book.
- Permitted: **one formula sheet, A4, one side, handwritten or printed, formulas and definitions only — no
  worked examples**. The sheet is collected with the exam.
- Simple calculator permitted; no graphing or programmable calculators.

## Topics

1. Sample spaces, events, axioms of probability; inclusion–exclusion.
2. Conditional probability, independence, Bayes' theorem.
3. Combinatorics: sampling with and without replacement.
4. Discrete random variables: Bernoulli, Binomial, Geometric, Poisson.
5. Continuous random variables: Uniform, Exponential, Normal.
6. Expectation, variance, covariance; linearity; variance of a sum.
7. Law of large numbers; Central Limit Theorem and the normal approximation (with continuity correction).
8. Markov and Chebyshev inequalities.
9. Joint distributions and conditional expectation (introduction).
