# Extension-request draft

When the overload warning shows a deadline that cannot be met, offer — once — a draft message asking for an
extension. The student reviews, edits and sends it. The skill never sends it, never contacts staff, and never
invents a reason.

## Rules

- **The reason is the student's.** Ask what it is (reserve duty, illness, a clash of exams, a family event,
  workload) if they have not said. Do not embellish, do not add a medical detail, do not write "as you know".
- **Check the course rules first.** Many courses publish an extension policy (a form, a TA, a fixed number of
  late days, מילואים rules). If `COURSE.md` or the syllabus says how, the draft follows that channel.
- **Specific and short**: course and assignment name, the original due date, the date asked for, the reason in
  one sentence, what is already done, an offer of an alternative (partial submission on time, a shorter extension).
- **Language** of the course's administration (`COURSE.md: language`; Hebrew by default in Israeli universities).
  Hebrew drafts follow the `hebrew-with-english` rules and pass `check_rtl.py`.
- **Polite, not apologetic**: no grovelling, no excessive thanks, no emojis.
- Reserve duty (מילואים) in Israel usually carries formal rights; say once that the student can attach the
  approval (אישור מילואים) and that the university's dean-of-students office handles these requests.

## Template (Hebrew)

> שלום [שם המתרגל/ת או המרצה],
> אני [שם], סטודנט/ית בקורס [שם הקורס] ([מספר הקורס]).
> אני מבקש/ת הארכה להגשת [שם המטלה], שמועד ההגשה שלה [תאריך], עד [תאריך מבוקש].
> הסיבה: [משפט אחד, במילים של הסטודנט/ית].
> עד עכשיו השלמתי את [מה שכבר נעשה], ואם אפשר, אגיש את החלק הזה בזמן.
> תודה,
> [שם]

## Template (English)

> Dear [name],
> I am [name], a student in [course] ([code]). I am writing to ask for an extension on [assignment], due
> [date], until [requested date]. The reason: [one sentence, in the student's words]. I have already completed
> [what is done] and can submit that part on time if that helps.
> Best regards,
> [name]
