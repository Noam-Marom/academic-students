# MLA 9th edition

Author–page. Literature, languages, cultural studies.

## In-text and list rules

- In text: (Smith 45), (Smith and Lee 45), (Smith et al. 45); no comma, no "p.".
- Works Cited alphabetical, hanging indent, titles in title case.
- Containers: the larger work (journal, website) in italics, followed by its elements in order.
- Hebrew works: Hebrew title as published; add a translation in square brackets when the course asks.

## Examples

1. Smith, John. *Title of Book*. Publisher, 2020.
2. Smith, John, and Kyung Lee. "Title of Article." *Journal Name*, vol. 12, no. 3, 2020, pp. 45–67.
3. Katz, Ruth. "A Chapter." *The Book*, edited by Jane Doe, Press, 2018, pp. 10–20.
4. "A Page." *Site Name*, 4 May 2021, example.org/p.
5. Smith, John, et al. "A Collaborative Article." *Journal Name*, vol. 9, 2016, pp. 1–9.
6. כהן, אבי. *שם הספר*. הוצאה, 2019.

## Checks

- Every in-text citation ⇄ list entry: `scripts/check_citations.py`.
- Entries with fields from the student's own source only; an entry that cannot be resolved (DOI, catalogue, database) is kept and marked `[unverified]`, never completed from memory.
