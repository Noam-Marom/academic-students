# Default rubric (when the course gives none)

Used by `feedback` mode when the assignment, `COURSE.md` and the genre file give no rubric. Say that it is
the plugin's default and that the course's own criteria override it. Score each criterion 1–4; the total
is reported as a percentage with the weights below. Five criteria × four levels:

| Criterion (weight) | 4 — excellent | 3 — good | 2 — developing | 1 — insufficient |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Question and thesis (25%)** | precise, arguable question; thesis on page 1 and carried throughout | clear question and thesis, sometimes lost in the body | topic rather than question; thesis vague | no identifiable question or thesis |
| **Argument and analysis (30%)** | every section advances the thesis; counter-arguments answered | mostly analytical, some description | mainly descriptive; analysis thin | summary only |
| **Use of sources (20%)** | relevant scholarly sources, compared and evaluated, accurately cited | adequate sources, mostly accurate citation | few or weak sources; citation errors | sources missing, unverifiable or misrepresented |
| **Structure and genre conventions (15%)** | follows the genre's structure (`references/genres/`); headings and transitions guide the reader | structure mostly clear | structure partly missing or confusing | no recognisable structure |
| **Language and presentation (10%)** | academic register, precise, error-free; format and citation style consistent | minor language or format slips | frequent errors that distract | errors impede understanding |

Feedback format: the score table, then **the ten most valuable fixes** ordered by expected gain in grade
(each: where, what, why, how to fix — without rewriting the passage), then what already works. In a DOCX,
the fixes go in as margin comments in a copy of the file; the original is never modified.
