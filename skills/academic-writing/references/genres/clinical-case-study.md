# Clinical case study (תיאור מקרה קליני)

A structured presentation of a (fictional or fully anonymised) patient: history, findings, reasoning, management and learning points; SOAP in medicine, the nursing process (ADPIE) in nursing.

## Structure

- Introduction: why the case is instructive
- Presentation: history (S), examination and investigations (O), with units and reference ranges
- Assessment: differential diagnosis ranked, with reasoning
- Plan: investigations, treatment, monitoring, education
- Outcome and discussion against the literature
- Learning points; consent / anonymisation statement

## What examiners reward

- Clinical reasoning made explicit
- Correct use of terminology and units
- Evidence-based management with sources
- Patient privacy protected completely
- Honest discussion of what went wrong or was uncertain

## Common mistakes

1. Identifiable details (dates, ward, age + rare condition)
2. A differential with no reasoning
3. Drug doses without units or route
4. Literature discussion unrelated to the case's question
5. Learning points that are generic

## Hebrew and English

Write in the submission language. Hebrew papers keep English terms in parentheses at first mention and follow `references/hebrew-academic.md`; English papers by Hebrew speakers get a pass with `references/english-academic.md`.
