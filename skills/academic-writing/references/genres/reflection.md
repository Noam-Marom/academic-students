# Reflective writing (רפלקציה)

First-person writing about an experience (placement, project, course) that turns description into learning; common in education, nursing, social work, design. The experience and its meaning are the student's own.

## Structure

- Context: what happened, briefly
- Description of a specific moment
- Feelings and thoughts at the time
- Analysis: linked to course concepts or literature
- What was learned
- Action plan: what will be done differently

## What examiners reward

- Specific moments rather than summaries
- Honesty, including about mistakes
- Theory used to explain the experience
- Change in understanding shown, not claimed
- A concrete action plan

## Common mistakes

1. Narrating the whole experience with no analysis
2. Only positive self-description
3. Theory dropped in without connection
4. Generic lessons ("I learned teamwork is important")
5. Writing what the lecturer wants to hear instead of what happened

## Hebrew and English

Write in the submission language. Hebrew papers keep English terms in parentheses at first mention and follow `references/hebrew-academic.md`; English papers by Hebrew speakers get a pass with `references/english-academic.md`.
