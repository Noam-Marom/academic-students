# Literature review (סקירת ספרות)

A synthesis of what is known on a question: what the field agrees on, where it disagrees, and what is missing. Stand-alone or the second chapter of a paper or thesis.

## Structure

- Introduction: scope, question, inclusion criteria (years, databases, languages)
- Thematic sections: each a theme or position, sources compared within it
- Synthesis table (optional): study, design, sample, finding
- Gaps and contradictions: what the literature does not answer
- Conclusion: what this means for the research question

## What examiners reward

- Organisation by theme, not by author
- Comparison and evaluation of sources (design, sample, quality)
- A stated search method
- An explicit gap that motivates the next study
- Recent and seminal sources both present

## Common mistakes

1. "Smith (2010) found… Jones (2012) found…" with no synthesis
2. Sources chosen to agree with the author
3. No search strategy, so the coverage cannot be judged
4. Over-reliance on secondary summaries or textbooks
5. Gap claimed without showing it

## Hebrew and English

Write in the submission language. Hebrew papers keep English terms in parentheses at first mention and follow `references/hebrew-academic.md`; English papers by Hebrew speakers get a pass with `references/english-academic.md`.
