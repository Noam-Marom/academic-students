# Artist statement (הצהרת אמן)

A short first-person text (150–300 words) that says what a body of work is about, how it is made and why. The claims are the artist's own; the skill helps structure and language only.

## Structure

- Opening: what the work is (medium, subject) in one sentence
- Concerns: the questions or ideas it explores
- Process: materials, methods, decisions
- Context: influences or traditions, briefly
- Closing: what the viewer is invited to do or notice

## What examiners reward

- Clarity and specificity over jargon
- A voice recognisably the artist's
- Consistency with the work shown
- Brevity
- Concrete description of process

## Common mistakes

1. Inflated theory ("interrogates the liminal") without meaning
2. Describing intentions the work does not show
3. A biography instead of a statement
4. Third person
5. Length beyond the brief

## Hebrew and English

Write in the submission language. Hebrew papers keep English terms in parentheses at first mention and follow `references/hebrew-academic.md`; English papers by Hebrew speakers get a pass with `references/english-academic.md`.
