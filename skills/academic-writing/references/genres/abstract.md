# Abstract (תקציר)

A 150–300 word stand-alone summary of a paper, thesis or conference submission; often also required in the other language (Hebrew paper → English abstract).

## Structure

- Context and problem (1–2 sentences)
- Aim / research question
- Method
- Main results with numbers
- Conclusion and implication
- Keywords (3–6)

## What examiners reward

- Every section of the paper represented
- Concrete results, not "results are discussed"
- Stand-alone: no citations, no undefined abbreviations
- Within the word limit
- Consistency with the paper

## Common mistakes

1. Background taking half the abstract
2. No results
3. Claims not in the paper
4. Abbreviations undefined
5. Hebrew and English abstracts that say different things

## Hebrew and English

Write in the submission language. Hebrew papers keep English terms in parentheses at first mention and follow `references/hebrew-academic.md`; English papers by Hebrew speakers get a pass with `references/english-academic.md`.
