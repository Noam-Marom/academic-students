# Legal opinion / legal problem (חוות דעת משפטית)

An analysis of a set of facts under the law: the issues, the rules, the application and the conclusion (IRAC), often with the Israeli pattern שאלה משפטית → הדין → יישום → מסקנה.

## Structure

- Heading: to whom, from whom, subject, date (for an opinion memo)
- Facts as given (only the relevant ones)
- Issues: each legal question separately
- Rule per issue: statute section, binding precedent, pinpoint citations
- Application: facts to rule, both sides, the stronger argument and why
- Conclusion and recommendation, hedged as the law requires

## What examiners reward

- Every issue spotted, including the less obvious ones
- Precise rules with correct citation (hebrew-legal style)
- Application that uses the specific facts
- Counter-arguments taken seriously
- A clear bottom line

## Common mistakes

1. Restating the law without applying it
2. Merging separate issues into one
3. Citing a case for a rule it does not hold, or a case that cannot be verified
4. Ignoring facts that cut against the conclusion
5. A conclusion that does not answer the question asked

## Hebrew and English

Write in the submission language. Hebrew papers keep English terms in parentheses at first mention and follow `references/hebrew-academic.md`; English papers by Hebrew speakers get a pass with `references/english-academic.md`.
