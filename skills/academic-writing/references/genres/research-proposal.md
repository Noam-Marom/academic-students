# Research proposal (הצעת מחקר)

A plan for a study the student will carry out (seminar, thesis, grant): what will be asked, why, how, and whether it is feasible.

## Structure

- Title and abstract
- Background and literature: the gap
- Research questions and hypotheses
- Method: design, participants/materials, procedure, measures, analysis plan
- Ethics (IRB / ועדת אתיקה), timeline, budget if required
- Expected contribution and limitations
- References

## What examiners reward

- Questions that follow from the gap
- A method that can actually answer the questions
- Feasibility: time, access, sample size justified
- Ethics addressed concretely
- A realistic timeline

## Common mistakes

1. Questions too broad to answer with the proposed method
2. No power analysis or sample-size reasoning
3. Analysis plan missing or "will be decided later"
4. Ethics as one sentence
5. Background that does not lead to the question

## Hebrew and English

Write in the submission language. Hebrew papers keep English terms in parentheses at first mention and follow `references/hebrew-academic.md`; English papers by Hebrew speakers get a pass with `references/english-academic.md`.
